# 0011: Case-study extras, a sticky header, brand icons and performance

- **Status:** Accepted. Abel chose the case-study elements, the icon approach and the performance work on 2 October 2026,
  and asked for the favicon, preview images, page width, mobile and header to be improved. The details below are
  Claude's implementation, measured before and after.
- **Date:** 2 October 2026
- **Decides:** what case studies show beyond the original design, the header, icons and preview images, and the
  performance settings.
- **Relates to:** [0004](0004-a-page-per-project.md), [0007](0007-fontshare-erode-and-author.md),
  [0010](0010-gallery-picks.md).

## Decision

1. **Stat strip** (App Store style) only where real figures exist: Betmate (1M users, £500k jackpot, iOS + Android)
   and cabeazy (0% commission, £50 a month, 3 kinds of user). GPFlow and whenwillyoumarry have none, so no strip.
2. **Flow strip** (Mobbin flows): 3–4 of the project's own screens, numbered and joined by decorative arrows, in a
   keyboard-scrollable ordered list.
3. **"On this page"** (Linear docs) on screens 1280px and wider, marking the section being read.
4. **Header:** sticky on paper with the AG monogram and a "Book a call" pill; `scroll-padding-top` keeps anchors and
   focused elements clear of it. Content width rises from 1120px to 1280px; paragraphs keep their own measure.
5. **Icons:** the favicon and Apple touch icon are the AG monogram in Erode, generated by `next/og`; the old "Abel."
   SVG is gone. Contact, file and technology icons come from simple-icons (CC0), gilbarbara/logos (CC0) and Tabler
   (MIT); a technology without an exact mark shows as text, never a stand-in (C# once wore the "sharp" library's mark).
6. **Preview images:** home shows the photo, name, role and headline; each case study shows its headline beside two
   real screens on the project's cover colour (`src/content/covers.js`).
7. **Performance:** Erode ships one weight (400); images are served as AVIF first; public images cache for a week.
   JetBrains Mono stays preloaded: removing it made the first screen's mono labels re-render late (live speed index
   1.8s to 3.0s). Local Lighthouse (mobile) on Betmate went from 89 to 98. Live scores vary by up to 10 points between
   runs, so live single runs are not used to judge small changes.
8. **Runtime:** no `output: "standalone"` (Railway runs `next start`); Node is pinned to 24.x, matching CI.
